Peterbot: Fortnite career 2024

 
 
 

Peterbot, whose real name is Peter Kata, is an American-Hungarian professional Fortnite player known for his aggressive playstyle, fast mechanics, high elimination counts and strong competitive results. He was born on June 20, 2007, and has competed at the highest levels of Fortnite esports. 

 

2024 — The breakthrough year

 

2024 was one of the biggest years of Peterbot's career.

  • FNCS Major 1: Peterbot and his duo partner Pollo finished second in the North American Grand Finals.

  • FNCS Major 2: They bounced back to win first place in North America.

  • FNCS Major 3: They won again, establishing themselves as one of the strongest duos in the region.

  • Esports World Cup 2024: Peterbot finished second in the Fortnite competition.

  • FNCS Global Championship 2024: Peterbot and Pollo won the world championship on September 8, 2024.

The Global Championship featured a $2 million prize pool, and Peterbot and Pollo took home the title after finishing ahead of Queasy and Th0masHD in second place. 

 
 

Why 2024 mattered: Peterbot proved that he could do more than dominate online matches. Winning the Global Championship established him as a world-class Fortnite competitor.
