2025 Peterbot

2025 — More tournament wins, tougher competition

 

Peterbot continued to perform at a high level in 2025, but the competitive landscape became more challenging.

His major achievements included:

  • FNCS Major 2: Won the North American Central Grand Finals with Ritual and Cold.

  • FNCS Pro-Am: Won the 2025 Pro-Am showmatch with AussieAntics.

  • FNCS Major 3: Won another North American Central Grand Finals with Ritual and Cold.

  • Fortnite Global Championship 2025: Finished 15th with Cold and Ritual, earning $24,000.

     

The 2025 Global Championship was played in trios rather than duos. Peterbot was no longer competing with Pollo as his teammate in that event.

He still showed his elimination ability, with his team recording 54 eliminations across the championship, the fourth-highest total. However, the team finished outside the top 10 overall. 

Why 2025 mattered: Peterbot demonstrated that his success was not limited to one championship-winning partnership. He kept winning regional tournaments, even though he did not repeat his 2024 world-title result.
